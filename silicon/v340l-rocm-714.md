## Compile `--offload-arch=gfx900`

Read `ROCm/llvm-project` `amd-staging`: `GCNProcessors.td` (`f2e9e8cf`), `AMDGPU.td`.

| Target | Mix / DOT |
|---|---|
| `gfx900` (`FeatureISAVersion9_0_0`) | **`FeatureMadMixInsts`** → `v_mad_mix_*` |
| `gfx906` (`FeatureISAVersion9_0_6`) | `FeatureFmaMixInsts` + DL DOT |
| `gfx9-generic` (`FeatureISAVersion9_Generic`) | **Neither.** Drops `v_mad_mix` |

`gfx9-generic` objects may load as a HIP fallback. They are the wrong ISA. **gfx906 objects will not load.** Compile `--offload-arch=gfx900` only.

## First success on iron (8× V340L)

Not tok/s. In order:

1. `lspci -nn` → **16** functions, `1002:6864`. `LnkSta` Gen3 x16 per card (two dies share it). BAR0 ≈ **8G/die**, not a V620 32G.
2. `rocminfo` → **`gfx900` × 16**.
3. `hipcc --offload-arch=gfx900` hello. **`hipModuleLoad` of a `v_mad_mix` object.** That is the adopted gate.
4. ISA dump: **`v_mad_mix_*` / `v_pk_fma_f16`**. **No** `v_dot*`.
5. Tensile gfx900 kpack / one rocBLAS GEMM on device 0 **if the gfx900 `.dat` is present**. `rocblas_*gemm` returns 0; `hipDeviceSynchronize` clean.

Stop there. Dual-die scheduling, packing, and serving come after. hippih is the HIP lab; vLLM is Later.
