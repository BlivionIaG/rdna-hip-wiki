# Radeon PRO V340L — Vega10 gfx900, not gfx1030 / not gfx906

Date: 2026-08-19. **Later. Own host.** Not today's vLLM/V620 work. Path lock elsewhere: **vLLM fork first, then Llaminar** (SGLang demoted).

Companion: [plx-p2p-mmio.md](plx-p2p-mmio.md) (two 5-slot 88096s, 8× V620 on the gfx1030 host).

## Identity

Linux amdgpu groups V340L with Vega 10 (RX Vega / WX Vega / V320 / 8200 / 9100 / SSG MxGPU). LLVM target **gfx900** (also gfx902/909/90c family for the MAD_MIX docs).

Public V340 material: **dual Vega 10** on one PCIe **3.0** x16 card, 56 CUs/die (3584 SP), HBM2 (commonly 16 GB/die → 32 GB/card), MxGPU/SR-IOV. One card = **two HIP devices**.

Not RDNA2 gfx1030. Not Vega 20 gfx906 (MI50 / Radeon VII / Llaminar's current ROCm backend).

## Same-host ban (locked 2026-08-19)

**8× V340L incoming go in their own machine.** Do not seat them on the two 88096 / 8× V620 host.

| Reason | Detail |
|---|---|
| HSA MMIO | Pre-gfx1030 AMD cards in the same host break remapped MMIO (`Failed to map remapped mmio page`). Toolbox rule; V340L is Vega 10. |
| ROCm 7.2 | Official Linux table is **V620**, not gfx900. Mixed KFD on one IOMMU domain is the trap. |
| Slots | Two 5-slot 88096s = **10** slots. 8 V620 + 8 V340L = **16** cards. |
| ISA | V620 objects are `fdot2`/`sdot4`. V340L objects are `mad_mix`/`pk_fma`. Fatbins are not interchangeable ([hippih.md](hippih.md)). |

hippih may grow a gfx900 TU **later**, on the V340L host, not as a 9th device in the V620 PIX mesh.

## PCIe on the 88096 backplanes (if someone plugs one anyway)

The backplanes are Gen4. The **endpoint** is Gen3.

| Field | Expected |
|---|---|
| `LnkCap` on the 88096 DSP | Gen4 x16 (switch) |
| **`LnkSta` on the V340L** | **8 GT/s × 16** — endpoint-limited, not a board defect |
| Payload ceiling | **15.754 GB/s** (same as 8749 x16), shared by **both dies** on the card |
| Devices per slot | **2** HIP devices (dual Vega 10). Intra-card hop is the card's own bridge, not the 88096 |
| BAR0 per die | typically **16G** HBM2 (confirm on `lspci`; not a V620 32G) |

So: even a “working” V340L in an 88096 slot is a **Gen3 DSP**. It does not join a V620 PIX ring at 31.5 GB/s. Two dies sharing one x16 also halves any naive per-GPU bus budget.

`lspci -vv` **LnkSta**, not **LnkCap**.

## HIP / ISA — what is actually available

### gfx900 (V340L) — keep

LLVM `AMDGPUAsmGFX900` VOP3P extras:

| Opcode | Role |
|---|---|
| `v_mad_mix_f32` | fp16×fp16→fp32 mix-MAD into fp32 accum |
| `v_mad_mixlo_f16` / `v_mad_mixhi_f16` | mix-MAD with fp16 result lane |
| `v_pk_fma_f16` | packed fp16 FMA (core GFX9 VOP3P; two fp16 lanes) |

Plus ordinary `v_fma_f32`, `v_pk_*` integer/fp16 ALU, wave64. This is the legal inner loop for an unquantized or dequant→fp16 GEMM on V340L: **mix-MAD / packed FMA**, not DOT.

HIP/LLVM will not emit `__builtin_amdgcn_fdot2` / `sdot4` / `sdot8` for `-mcpu=gfx900`.

### gfx906 (Vega 20) — not on this card

LLVM `AMDGPUAsmGFX906` adds the DL DOT family and switches mix to FMA:

| Opcode | On V340L? |
|---|---|
| `v_dot2_f32_f16` (`fdot2`) | **no** |
| `v_dot4_i32_i8` (`sdot4`) | **no** |
| `v_dot8_i32_i4` (`sdot8`) | **no** |
| `v_fma_mix_f32` (replaces `mad_mix` on gfx906) | **no** (gfx900 keeps `mad_mix`) |

Old notes that say “fdot2 on gfx9+” are **wrong for Vega10**. DOT starts at **gfx906** in the LLVM asm tables used here.

### Consequence for Llaminar / our fork

- Llaminar ROCm is **gfx906-only** today. Its DOT objects will **not** load on V340L.
- Our V620 `fdot2`/`sdot4`/`sdot8` objects will **not** load either.
- A V340L port needs a separate gfx900 TU: `mad_mix` / `pk_fma_f16`, wave64, 64 KB LDS/CU, dual-die scheduling.

## What else breaks vs V620 kernels

| Assumption on V620 | V340L |
|---|---|
| wave32 WGP | wave64 CU |
| `fdot2` / `sdot4` / `sdot8` | `mad_mix` + `pk_fma_f16` only |
| 128 KB LDS/WGP, 64 KB/WG | 64 KB LDS/CU |
| PCIe 4.0, 512 GB/s GDDR6 | PCIe 3.0, HBM2, two GPUs share one x16 |
| gfx10 occupancy knobs | different VGPR/wave accounting |

gfx1100 W7800 WMMA kernels are equally non-portable.

## If we ever bench it (on its own host)

1. Confirm `rocminfo` → `gfx900` and two devices per card.
2. Do not reuse gfx1030 or gfx906 fatbins.
3. ISA-dump any “fp16 GEMM” and require `v_mad_mix_*` / `v_pk_fma_f16`, not `v_dot*`.
4. Dual-die + shared PCIe 3.0 x16 changes any P2P / activation-A2A budget. Expect `LnkSta` Gen3 even in an 88096 slot.
5. ROCm 7.x support for Vega 10 is not assumed; record the exact stack.
6. Never co-install with the V620 ROCm 7 host.

Today: ignore V340L in occupancy, HIP MoE, and vLLM fork work.
